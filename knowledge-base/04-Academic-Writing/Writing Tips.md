# Writing Tips

Effective strategies for academic and research writing.

## Structure
- **Clear Thesis**: Every paper should have a clear, arguable central claim.
- **Logical Flow**: Ensure sections transition smoothly with topic sentences.

## Process
- **Outline First**: Map out your argument before writing prose.
- **Iterative Drafting**: Write fast first drafts; revise for clarity and precision.
