# ChatRipe Knowledge Base

This directory is a standard **Obsidian Vault**. You can open it directly in [Obsidian](https://obsidian.md/) to manage your research notes, project ideas, and academic references.

## How to use with Obsidian
1. Open the Obsidian application.
2. Click on "Open folder as vault".
3. Select this `knowledge-base/` folder.

## Folder Structure
- `00-Inbox`: Quick captures and unsorted notes.
- `01-Research`: Primary research notes and literature reviews.
- `02-AI-and-Machine-Learning`: Specific technical notes on AI/ML.
- `03-Programming`: Coding snippets, tutorials, and documentation.
- `04-Academic-Writing`: Drafts, outlines, and writing tips.
- `05-Projects`: Project-specific notes and tasks.
- `06-References`: PDFs, images, and bibliography files.
- `99-Archive`: Completed projects and deprecated notes.

## Integration
This knowledge base is linked to the ChatRipe AI Research Chatbot. You can eventually use these notes as a source for Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG) to ground your AI assistant's responses in your own personal knowledge.
