# Research Methodology

Research methodology refers to the specific procedures or techniques used to identify, select, process, and analyze information about a topic.

## Qualitative vs. Quantitative
- **Qualitative**: Focuses on words and meanings.
- **Quantitative**: Focuses on numbers and statistics.
